Privacy Policy for Placeling

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Placeling (“the game”, “the app”) handles information. The game is made and published on Google Play by DP Games, a small independent game studio based in South Africa (“DP Games”, “we”, “I”, “the developer”).

1. Who to contact

Developer: DP Games (South Africa)
Privacy contact: drikusraath@gmail.com

Email me about anything in this policy, including questions, requests about your data, or complaints. I aim to reply within 30 days.

2. Summary

  • The game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.
  • The game works fully offline. It does not need an account or a sign-in, and it does not ask for any device permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device, in the game’s private app storage. They are never sent to me or to anyone else.

4. Sharing of information

I don’t sell, rent or trade personal information. The game doesn’t collect any personal information, so there’s nothing to share.

5. Children

The game is meant for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you’re a parent or guardian and think your child has sent me personal information (for example by email), contact me at the address above and I’ll delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Game progress and settings stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app’s storage (Android Settings > Apps > Placeling > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the game. I keep no copy.
  • Emails to me are kept only as long as described in section 3.
  • The game has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete.

7. Security

Because the game stores your progress only in its private storage on your device, other apps can’t normally read it. The game has no network features. No method of storage is 100% secure, but I follow Android’s standard security practices.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including:

  • South Africa (POPIA, Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013): the right to know whether I hold personal information about you and to get a copy; to ask me to correct or delete it; to object to its processing (including for direct marketing); and to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).
  • EEA / UK (GDPR and UK GDPR): the rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Other places (for example US states such as California): similar rights to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. I don’t sell personal information.

I hold no personal information about players apart from any emails you send me, so most requests can be met right away. To use any of these rights, email me at the contact address above. I won’t treat you differently for using your rights.

9. International transfers

The game doesn’t transfer personal information anywhere.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information (for example by adding ads or in-app purchases), I’ll update this policy before that version is released and change the “Last updated” date at the top. For significant changes I’ll also give notice in the game or on its Google Play page. Older versions are available on request.
